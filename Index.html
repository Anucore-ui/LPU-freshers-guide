Where should I start?

Quick answers to common fresher problems.

📚 Classes & timetable

Check UMS first. If information is missing, contact your mentor or programme coordinator.

🏠 Hostel issue

Contact your hostel warden or residential services desk with your room details.

🏥 Medical assistance

Visit the campus health centre. For an immediate emergency, call 112.

💳 Fee concern

Check the fee section in UMS and save your payment receipt.

🔐 Login or ID issue

Use the relevant UMS support option or visit the student help desk.

🔎 Lost an item?

Inform the nearest security desk and mention the location and time.

Important: University contacts can change. Always verify department, hostel and security numbers through the latest official LPU information.
