Emergency Help

If you are in immediate danger or facing a serious emergency, get help immediately.

🚨 National Emergency

For police, fire, ambulance or another urgent emergency.

CALL 112

🏥 Medical Emergency

If someone needs urgent medical assistance, seek campus medical support and call 112 when necessary.

Call Emergency Services

🛡️ Safety / Security

If you face a serious safety issue, alert the nearest campus security desk and seek assistance.

💬 Student Support

For student well-being and support, use the appropriate university support channel.

Call Student Support
Important: Do not delay contacting emergency services when there is an immediate danger. Campus SOS is an independent student project and does not replace official emergency services.
